private-equity-venture-capital, financial-services, professional-services,

### The article-to-magnet pairings, which already exist on the site

| Article | Its magnet |
|---|---|
| `/insights/ai-usage-policy-uk-template` | `/resources/ai-policy` |
| `/insights/prompts-your-team-will-reuse` | `/resources/prompt-library` |
| `/insights/measure-ai-adoption-three-numbers` | `/resources/week-four` |

Send people to the article, not the magnet, roughly two times in three. The article does
the persuading and carries the magnet at the end, so the person who downloads has already
decided it is worth having. A cold link to a form converts worse and reads as an advert.

**The industry pages are the most under-used asset here.** Eight sector pages exist and
nothing has ever linked to them. Any post about construction, retail, professional
services or the public sector should link to its sector page rather than the homepage.
That is both a better landing page and a genuine SEO signal.

## 2. The rotation

Across a rolling 21 posts, three a day for a week:

| Destination | Posts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Nothing at all** | 9 | The wide band. A page where every post carries a link reads as a funnel |
| **An insights article** | 5 | The default link. Six articles, so no article twice in a fortnight |
| **A sector page** | 3 | Matched to the story's industry |
| **`/prompts`** | 2 | Ungated, so it can run more often than the magnets |
| **A lead magnet** | 2 | One of the three, rotating |

**Lead magnet rotation.** One magnet per week, in order, so each is promoted roughly
every three weeks:

- Week 1: `/resources/ai-policy`
- Week 2: `/resources/prompt-library`
- Week 3: `/resources/week-four`

Never two magnet posts in the same day. Never the same magnet twice in a fortnight.
The daily task reads `destination` from the ledger to enforce both.

## 3. How a promotional post is written

The rule that makes these work: **give away the substance in the post, and let the PDF be
the convenience.** The reader should be able to act on the post without downloading
anything. The download is for people who want it printed, shareable and complete.

That sounds like it would reduce downloads. It does the opposite, because a post that
withholds reads as an advert and gets scrolled, while a post that delivers earns the
click from people who now trust that the PDF is worth having.

**The shape, four parts:**

1. **The problem, specifically.** "Most AI policies fail because nobody read them." Not
   "AI governance is important."
2. **The actual answer, most of it.** The four questions a one page policy answers. The
   three numbers to count at week four. Five of the twenty five prompts. Real content,
   not a table of contents.
3. **One sentence naming the artefact**, plainly. "The full template is three pages,
   seven clauses, free at aiforcompanies.co.uk/resources/ai-policy."
4. **Stop.** No urgency, no "limited", no "grab yours". Nothing that would embarrass us.

**Never:** "comment POLICY and I'll DM it", countdowns, "only for the next 24 hours",
"DM me for the link", or any claim about how many people have downloaded it. The first is
downranked by LinkedIn, and the rest are things a training company with no delivery
history should not be saying.

**The carousel version.** The three carousels already map to the three magnets, so a
carousel is the strongest promotional format available: it gives the whole framework
across six slides and names the PDF on the last one. Use these as the magnet posts rather
than writing new ones, and re-cut them with a different cover line each cycle.

## 4. Establishing the site, the part that is not social

Three things worth doing that the content engine cannot do for you, in order of effect
on how the site is seen:

1. **Link the sector pages from the posts.** Eight pages with no inbound links are
   invisible. Three posts a week pointing at them changes that.
2. **Put the LinkedIn page in the site footer and the delivery emails.** Free, permanent,
   and it works in both directions.
3. **`/resources/` has no index page.** The individual magnets exist and are live, but
   `aiforcompanies.co.uk/resources` is a 404, and the llms.txt does not list any of them.
   That means the three magnets are invisible to search and to AI crawlers, which is a
   real loss given they are the best assets on the site. Worth an index page and a
   regenerated llms.txt.

That third one is the highest-value fix on this list and it is a small piece of work.
