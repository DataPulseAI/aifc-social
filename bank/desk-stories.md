strategy) that he is happy to tell in public, each in one or two sentences, with the lesson it
carries for AI at work now. The Tuesday slot on the profile draws one a week. Filled from a call
or a voice note; the batch never invents one.

Format: `- <story> | <lesson> | (blank, or "used <post_id>")`
