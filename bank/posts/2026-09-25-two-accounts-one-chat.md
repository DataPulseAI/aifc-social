ChatGPT will now hold your work account and your personal one in the same conversation.

From OpenAI's release notes of 17 September: users can connect multiple accounts to plugins, beyond the Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts connections that already existed, so a personal and a work account can both be reachable inside a single conversation.

It solves a real irritation. Anyone who has ever had a meeting in one calendar and the person's details in the other knows why this shipped.

It also quietly removes a wall that a lot of firms were relying on without ever writing it down. The separation between work email and personal email was never a control. It was a side effect of the two being in different tabs. When both are answerable in one chat window, the side effect goes.

Nothing here needs banning. What it needs is a decision, made once, by somebody: does client information ever get pulled into a conversation that also has a personal account attached to it, and who is that person's answer binding on.

For a regulated firm that question already has an answer somewhere in your policies. This is the week to check the answer still matches what the tools can do.
