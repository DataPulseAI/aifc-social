The delivery's late again, and the email to the supplier goes into the assistant as six words.

What comes back is polite, vague and could be about anyone's delivery. So it gets rewritten by hand, and the assistant gets the blame.

Everything it was missing was already in your head: which delivery, when it was due, what you need from them and by when. Type that in instead: "Write an email to [supplier] about the delivery due on [date] that hasn't arrived. Ask for a new date by Friday and say what we need it for. Under 80 words. Firm, not rude. Where you don't know something, write CHECK." The draft then needs a read rather than a rewrite.

Read it once for the date and the name before it goes. Works in ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini, whichever you have.
