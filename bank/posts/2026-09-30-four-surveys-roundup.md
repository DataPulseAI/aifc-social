Four surveys asked how many UK small businesses use AI. They came back with 20%, 35%, 47% and 48%.

Enterprise Nation, 9 June 2026. "Only one in five of the UK's 5.7 million small businesses are using AI regularly." Their framing is that real use "remains at an early stage", and they set it against Denmark at 42% among young enterprises. The article does not state a sample size.
https://www.enterprisenation.com/learn-something/one-in-five-small-businesses-regularly-use-ai-new-enterprise-nation-research-finds/

Office for National Statistics, 20 July 2026. Around 35% of businesses with 10 or more employees use at least one AI technology, up from around 12% in late 2023. 28% among firms with fewer than 10 staff, 49% at 250 or more. From the Business Insights and Conditions Survey, wave 159, 38,637 responses.
https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026

Simply Business, 8 September 2026. 47%, up from 22% the year before. 1,842 small business owners, surveyed 30 July to 7 August.
https://www.simplybusiness.co.uk/knowledge/business-news/small-business-ai-usage-doubles/

Small Business Institute and Accio, 17 September 2026. 48% use AI regularly, from 1,000 owners in August. Commissioned by an AI company, which is worth knowing.

They are not contradicting each other. They are counting different firms, asking different questions and using the word regularly to mean different things. The ONS number is the one with the method behind it and the narrowest question.

None of which tells you anything about your own place. The number that decides whether the money was well spent is how many of your people opened the thing last week, and you can count that this afternoon.
