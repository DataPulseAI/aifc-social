The ONS asked UK businesses what AI had done to their workforce. Most of them reported no change to their overall headcount.

Among businesses using AI to improve their operations, around 6% reported a decrease. That is the figure, from the July release, and it sits a long way from the number implied by most of what you will have read about this in the last month.

We are not going to tell you the question is settled. The survey covers businesses with 10 or more employees, and it asks what has happened rather than what is planned. A firm that quietly did not replace a leaver will not always describe that as a decrease.

What the figure does undercut is the version where you are already behind and the cuts have already happened somewhere else. On what UK businesses are actually reporting, they have not.

So if someone is selling you AI on headcount savings, ask them for the same number from a UK source with a date on it. The honest answer is that the productivity case is easier to evidence than the redundancy one, and it is also the one your team will help you with rather than resist.

Source: ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses, 2023 to 2026, published 20 July 2026. https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
