Three changes to ChatGPT and Google worth trying.

ChatGPT. You can now lock a chat so it only opens with a 4-digit PIN or your device's own sign-in check, and it drops out of your chat list and search. It's rolling out on Free, Go, Plus and Pro. Open the chat's three-dot menu and choose Lock chat. It hides the chat. It doesn't delete it or change how your data is kept. (OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes, 20 October 2026)

Google Docs. If your firm is on Business Standard or Plus, Docs Live in the Docs app on your phone turns what you say into a structured document. Walk back from a site visit, open Docs and talk through what was agreed. (Google Workspace Updates, 21 October 2026)

Google Sheets. Gemini now works in Sheets on iPhone, on Business Starter, Standard and Plus. Open a spreadsheet and tap Ask Gemini, then ask which customers owe the most. (Google Workspace Updates, 19 October 2026)

If you only try one, try the second. Read the document before it goes anywhere.
