AI is a keen new starter. Quick, confident, eager to please, and every so often it makes something up with a straight face.

You wouldn't hand a new starter a job with no background and sign off whatever came back. Same here. Tell it what the job is, who it's for, and what good looks like. Then check anything with a number, a name or a date in it before it leaves the building.

That's most of the training, in a sentence.
