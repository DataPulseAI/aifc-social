Four changes to tools you already pay for, with the dates and what to do about each.

Google Meet, note taking, in effect since 29 September 2026. End users can now switch on "Take notes for me" for meetings with three or more participants. Decide before your next call whether that is on by default at your place, and tell people, because a meeting that is being minuted by software and has not said so is a conversation you will have once.
https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/

OpenAI, custom GPTs retiring, announced 11 September 2026. Custom GPTs are being migrated to plugins, with access continuing until retirement. If anyone at your firm built one that a team relies on, that is a job with a deadline attached rather than a notification to dismiss.
https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes

OpenAI, Privacy Center, 21 September 2026. Controls for chats, memory, personalisation and connected apps now sit in one place. Worth ten minutes with whoever administers your workspace, because the settings were always there and nobody could find them.

Microsoft, Copilot Chat in Outlook, general availability October 2026. It reasons over the inbox and calendar. The roadmap entry has been open since February, so this is the one to expect rather than the one to plan around.
https://www.microsoft.com/releasecommunications/api/v2/m365/rss

Of the four, the Meet change is the one already live.
