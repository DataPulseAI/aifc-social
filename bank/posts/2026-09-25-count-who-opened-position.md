Before you buy another AI licence, count who opened the last one.

Not who was given a seat. Who opened it in the past thirty days, and what for. Microsoft, OpenAI and Google all report usage to whoever administers the plan. Where the report sits depends on the product, but it exists, and almost nobody reads it before the renewal.

Our read is that the number will be lower than you expect, and that a second product will not raise it. A team that is not opening the assistant it has is not waiting for a better assistant. It is waiting for a reason, a first task, and someone to ask when it goes wrong. None of those come in a box.

The exception is real. A firm on a plan that cannot see its own documents, or that sends data somewhere the firm's contracts do not allow, may need a different licence. That is a data question and it has a yes-or-no answer. It is not the reason most second licences get bought.

What we would do. Pull the usage report this week, sort by last active, and talk to the three people at the bottom before talking to any vendor. Ask what they tried, what happened and why they stopped. Then decide whether the problem is the tool.
