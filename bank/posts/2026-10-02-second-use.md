Your team has one use for the assistant. Here are eight more, and none of them need a new tool.

The ONS has the average UK business using AI for around 1.6 things. The second use is where the money is, and it almost never arrives on its own, because the first use was discovered by accident and nobody went looking for a second.

Eight to hand someone this week. Each one is a task they already do, and each one takes a document they already have.

1. Turn last month's board pack into six questions a non-executive would ask.
2. Read a supplier contract and list every obligation that falls on you, with the clause number.
3. Take the notes from a meeting nobody wrote up and produce the actions with owners.
4. Rewrite a standard client email as three versions, for a new client, a late payer and a complaint.
5. Compare two quotes line by line and mark what one includes that the other does not.
6. Turn a spreadsheet you inherited into a plain description of what it calculates.
7. Draft the job advert from the last three people who did the job well.
8. Read your own website page and list what a buyer still would not know.

Give one person two of these, not eight. Ask them on Friday which was worth repeating, and add that one to the list everybody gets.

Source: ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses, 2023 to 2026, published 20 July 2026. https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
