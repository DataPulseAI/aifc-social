Nobody has ever remembered exactly what was agreed on a phone call.

You put the phone down certain. Nine days later the other person is equally certain, about something slightly different, and neither of you wrote anything down at the time.

Type your scribbles in as they are and ask: "Turn these notes from a phone call into a short email confirming what we agreed. List anything I said I'd do and anything they said they'd do. Where my notes don't say, write CHECK." Then read it, fill in the CHECKs, and send it while the call is still fresh.

What makes it work is that both of you have now seen the same dated record.

This is not legal advice.
