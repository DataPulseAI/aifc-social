Four things Google changed in Workspace this month. One of them needs a decision rather than a look.

Drive sharing rules, 16 September. Administrators can now set audience sharing and sensitivity conditions in a single unified rule, which Google frames as mitigating insider risk. If your sharing rules were written in two places and have drifted, this is the month to reconcile them.

Apps Script data regions, 16 September. Script storage and runtime execution now adhere to the region you specify, with controls for disabling non-regionalised services. Relevant if anyone has ever told a client where your data sits.

Notebooks in Gemini, 17 September. Available to organisations, keeping the material and the conversation about one topic in one place. This is the closest thing Google has shipped to the way people actually work on a project.

Workspace Studio, 17 September. Custom starters and steps, eight third-party integrations and webhooks, with granular security controls and approval settings.

That last one is the decision. Webhooks and third-party integrations mean someone in your organisation can now build an automation that reaches outside it, and the approval settings determine whether anyone finds out. Deciding who can approve takes ten minutes. Discovering the answer later takes considerably longer.
