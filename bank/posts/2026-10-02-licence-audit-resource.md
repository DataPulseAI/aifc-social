## Profile post (channel=profile, Friday, the resource shape, first person allowed)

I keep asking owners the same question and nobody can answer it.

How many of your AI seats were opened last month?

Not how many you pay for. How many were opened.

Every vendor already tells you. Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude all give an admin a per-seat report with last active date and active days.

So we wrote the hour that turns the report into a decision:

1. One row per seat. Name, tool, last active, active days in 28.
2. Three bands, decided before you see the names. Never, Tried, Uses.
3. One number for the board. Seats used over seats paid, as a fraction, monthly.

Then ask the Uses band one question: what do you use it for most? Three answers will be the same task. That is your team library, and nobody had to invent it.

The sheet, the report paths and the Monday actions are free: aiforcompanies.co.uk/resources/licence-audit
