Three changes in Gemini and Copilot worth a try.

Gemini. The Gemini app can now make a document, spreadsheet, slides or a PDF for you in the chat, and you can open it in Google Sheets or Slides to carry on. It's available on every Google Workspace plan and on personal Google accounts. Most file types work on the web for now; PDFs work on your phone too. Start small: ask it for a price list as a sheet. (Google Workspace Updates, 29 October 2026)

Google Slides. On Business Standard or Plus, one request in the Gemini side panel can update content across a whole deck. It's rolling out gradually, in English first. Open last year's deck and ask it to change every date. (Google Workspace Updates, 30 October 2026)

PowerPoint. If Copilot shows in your PowerPoint, you can attach an existing deck and it will use that deck's look for a new one. Pick the one your team already likes. (Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes, 27 October 2026)

If you only try one, try the first. It works on a personal Google account too.
