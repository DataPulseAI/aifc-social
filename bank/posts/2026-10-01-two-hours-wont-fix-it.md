Two hours of training will not move your team past one use of AI, and we sell training.

The ONS figure behind that is the one we keep returning to. The average UK business using AI uses around 1.6 AI technologies, up from around 1.4 since late 2023, while the share of businesses using anything at all went from around 12% to around 35%. A lot of firms started. Almost nobody went deeper.

A single session does not change that, and the reason is not the quality of the session. People leave a good one with four ideas and no slot in the week to try any of them. By Thursday the work that was already waiting has taken the time back, and the tool returns to the one task they had before.

What moves the number is narrower and duller. One task a person already does every week, done with the assistant, with someone named who owns it. Then a second task a fortnight later, chosen by the person doing it rather than by whoever bought the licence.

We would rather say this plainly than sell a day that produces a photograph and no second use. If you are going to run one session, book the follow-up before you run it, and make the follow-up about what people actually tried.

Source: ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses, 2023 to 2026, published 20 July 2026. https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
