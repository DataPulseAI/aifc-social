Six prompts in full, so you do not need the download to use them.

Every one of these takes a document you already have. Paste the prompt, attach the file, and read what comes back with the same suspicion you would give a new starter's first draft.

1. The contract read. "Read this agreement. List every obligation that falls on us, with the clause number. Mark anything with a date attached. Say which clauses are missing that you would expect in an agreement of this type." Attach the contract.

2. The quote comparison. "Compare these two quotes line by line. Produce a table of what each includes. Where one is silent on something the other covers, write not stated rather than guessing." Attach both.

3. The meeting actions. "From these notes, list the actions. Give each one an owner and a date. Where the notes do not say who owns something, write no owner." Attach the notes or transcript.

4. The board question set. "Read this pack. Write the eight questions a non-executive would ask, with the page each one comes from." Attach the pack.

5. The handover. "Explain in plain English what this spreadsheet calculates, what it takes as inputs, and what would break it." Attach the file.

6. The client update. "Draft this week's update from these notes. Four headings. Mark anything you are unsure of with CHECK." Attach the notes.

The pattern is the same in all six: give it a real document, tell it what to do when it does not know, and make the gaps visible instead of letting it fill them.
