Five estate agency jobs, and the exact words to type for each.

If you have opened ChatGPT twice and not known what to ask it, start here. These work in ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini, whichever you already have, and none of them needs anything you do not already own.

1. Viewing notes to a listing. Paste your notes, then type: "Turn these notes into a 120-word listing. Keep every measurement and price exactly as written."

2. Chasing the solicitor. "Write five lines asking for a progress update and a likely exchange date. Polite, not pushy."

3. A low offer lands. "Draft a note to the vendor: the offer, three options, and CHECK where you need a fact from me." The CHECK is the point. It stops the draft inventing a reason the buyer has gone in low.

4. Friday vendor update. Paste the week's viewings and feedback, then type: "Write the weekly update: viewings, what people said, next week's plan. No promises about price."

5. A pile of viewing feedback. "Group this feedback into themes, count each one, and list what we could fix this week."

Before any of it goes out, read every measurement, price and date against the original. A wrong measurement comes back sounding exactly as confident as a right one. And take the names out of anything you paste in. A vendor's name and address do not need to go near a chatbot to get a listing written.
