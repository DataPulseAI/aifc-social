41% of UK businesses with 10 or more employees told the ONS that no barrier at all is holding back their use of AI.

The same release puts the average number of AI technologies per business at around 1.6, up from around 1.4 since late 2023. Adoption over that period roughly tripled, from around 12% to around 35%. The width moved. The depth did not.

Put those two findings next to each other and the usual explanation stops working. If cost and skills and privacy were the thing in the way, the 41% would be a much smaller number. Most firms are not blocked. They have the tool, nothing is stopping them, and they are still using it for one or two things.

Our read is that this is what a training gap looks like from the outside. Nobody is standing in your team's way. Nobody has shown them the second use, or the third.

The cheapest test takes ten minutes. Ask five people what they used it for last week. If four of them say the same thing, you have found your 1.6.

Source: ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses, 2023 to 2026, published 20 July 2026. https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
