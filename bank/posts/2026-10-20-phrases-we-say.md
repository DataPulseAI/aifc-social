"I'll write the minutes later." Nobody has ever written the minutes later.

Every office runs on five or six phrases that all mean the same thing, which is that a job has been noticed and not done. The useful part is that each phrase is already a decent description of a task, which is most of the way to being something you can type.

So "I'll write the minutes later" becomes: "Turn these notes into a table: action, owner, deadline. Write no owner where nobody's named." The card has the other four.

Works in ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini, whichever you have. Check the names and the dates before you send anything on.
