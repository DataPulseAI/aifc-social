Four weeks in, three numbers tell you if AI stuck.

This works whether your team was trained, watched a video, or just got a login. You don't need a dashboard. The tool's own figures count people who are signed in, and being signed in isn't the same as using it.

One. Minutes saved on named jobs. Pick the five jobs you hoped AI would help with and ask how long each took this week. If you never took a before figure, take one today and count again in four weeks.

Two. Jobs still done with it. Count the things people started doing with AI in the first week, then how many anyone did that way this week. The ones that survived tell you what mattered.

Three. Real use. Ask in the team meeting who used it on real work this week, meaning work that went somewhere.

Then make one decision and write it down next to the numbers: roll it out to the next team, fix what got in the way, or stop.
