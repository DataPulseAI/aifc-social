Gmail search has started answering the question instead of listing the threads.

Google's Workspace Updates of 18 September: "Starting today, we are expanding access to AI Overviews in Gmail search to global users (with paid plans) who have their Gmail language set as English." It was the United States only until now. If your firm is on a paid Workspace plan and works in English, it is on.

A search box that returns a list and a search box that returns an answer are different products with the same shape, and the second one asks for a kind of trust the first never did. Nobody double-checks a list of threads, because the list is not making a claim. An answer is.

The useful version of this is not a policy. It is one sentence said once in a team meeting: the overview is a starting point, and anything going to a client or into a number gets the original opened.

That sentence costs nothing and it is the difference between a summary that saves ten minutes and a summary that quietly loses you a date, an amount or a name.
