Four Copilot changes with dates on them, so you can plan rather than react.

All four are on the Microsoft 365 Roadmap, read on 21 September 2026.

Power BI reports as references in Copilot Notebooks, generally available October 2026. Copilot can use a report as a reference when it generates a summary, which is the difference between an assistant that guesses at your numbers and one that reads them.

Deep citations in Copilot, generally available November 2026. Citations will link directly to the relevant part of the reference rather than to the document. Microsoft's own framing is that this is about trust, and it is the single change on this list most likely to affect whether people believe what they are shown.

Copilot Cowork for Government Clouds, generally available November 2026. Work is delegated by describing the outcome, grounded in emails, meetings, messages, files and data.

Purview unified classification management, generally available November 2026. Classifiers move into one place to discover, create, validate and manage.

The roadmap is worth ten minutes a month for one reason: it is the only place a vendor tells you what is coming before it appears in your tenant. Everything else you read is written after the fact.
