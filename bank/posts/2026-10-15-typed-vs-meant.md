"Write a reply to this complaint." Six words, and you wonder why it came back sounding like a bank.

Everything that makes a good reply was in your head, not in the box. Who the customer is, what went wrong, what you're offering, how long it should be.

Try it with the detail you'd give a colleague: "Reply to this complaint from a customer whose delivery was 3 days late. Apologise once, give the new date, offer free delivery on the next order, under 120 words, no jargon." Paste the complaint underneath.

Works the same in ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini, whichever you have.
