"Much of the adoption today has been at a surface level."

That is Michelle Lau, managing director of Alibaba.com, on research published on 17 September covering 1,000 UK small business owners. Worth saying plainly before anything else: the research was commissioned by an AI company, which is a reason to read the numbers carefully rather than a reason to ignore them.

The numbers are these. 70% say they are somewhat or very confident using generative AI. 48% use it regularly. Among those who do, 74% use it for content and marketing, 33% for data analysis or reporting, and 5% for anything to do with the supply chain.

So confidence is high, use is common, and the use is concentrated almost entirely in writing things.

That gap is the one we keep meeting. Writing is where everyone starts because it is the demonstration everyone has seen. It is also the use with the smallest return, because a faster first draft of an email saves minutes, and reconciling two lists that should match saves an afternoon.

The question that moves a team past the surface is not which tool. It is which job. Pick the task on your own week that takes longest and is least interesting, and give that one to the assistant before you give it another email.

Source: Small Business Institute and Accio, research of 1,000 UK small business owners, August 2026, published 17 September 2026.
