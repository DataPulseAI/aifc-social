Three small changes you'll actually see on your own screen.

ChatGPT. The camera can now scan several pages into one PDF, and you can ask questions about it. It's rolling out on iPhone. Next time a paper invoice lands on the counter, photograph it and ask what it says and what the total comes to. (OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes, 1 October 2026)

Copilot. If Copilot Chat shows in your browser, you can now ask it for a different answer rather than retyping the request. Useful on the drafts that come back nearly right. Ask for another go and say what was wrong with the first one. (Microsoft, Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes, 6 October 2026)

Gmail. If your team uses Trello, an email or a Google Chat message can now be turned into a Trello card. It's on all Google Workspace plans and personal Google accounts, and you need a Trello account. Good for the job that's been buried in a thread since Tuesday. (Google Workspace Updates, 6 October 2026)

If you only try one, try the first. Don't scan anything with someone's personal details on it into a free account.
