Three small changes you'll actually see on your own screen.

ChatGPT. Point the camera at several pages in a row and ChatGPT now combines them into one PDF, ready to drop into a chat. Open the camera, tap the three dots and pick Scan. It's rolling out on iPhone. Next time a paper invoice lands on the counter, scan it and ask what the total comes to. (OpenAI, ChatGPT release notes, 1 October 2026)

Copilot. If Copilot Chat shows in your browser, its answers now carry a Try Again option, so you can get a different answer without retyping the request. It sits under More options at the end of an answer, next to Switch Model. Useful on the drafts that come back nearly right. (Microsoft, Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes, 6 October 2026)

Gemini in Google Meet. If your firm is on Business Standard or Plus and you turn on Take notes for me during the call, the screen after it now offers to draft the follow-up email or add the actions to Google Tasks. Try it on your next supplier call and read the draft before it goes. (Google Workspace Updates, 12 October 2026)

If you only try one, try the first. Don't scan anything with someone's personal details on it into a free account, and tell people on a call before Meet takes notes.
