38% of UK businesses had a phishing attack in the last 12 months.

That's from the government's Cyber security breaches survey 2025/2026, published 30 April 2026. It surveyed 2,112 UK businesses between August and December 2025. Phishing was the most common attack by far, and the one most often named as the most disruptive.

Line up eight firms like yours, and three of them had one.

Being small doesn't help much. Counting any breach or attack, it was 42% of micro businesses and 46% of small ones.

Our read is that the cheapest defence for a firm your size is a rule everyone knows, not more software.

So agree one rule this week, and say it out loud at the next team meeting: any email asking to change bank details gets a phone call to a number you already have. Not the number in the email.

Source: DSIT and DCMS, Cyber security breaches survey 2025/2026, 30 April 2026. 2,112 UK businesses surveyed, August to December 2025. Official statistics; counts only the attacks firms identified and reported. https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/cyber-security-breaches-survey-20252026
