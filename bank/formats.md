# The seven formats

From docs/23 section 4. The batch writes each slot from the template below. A reader should
recognise the format before reading it. Nothing here overrides docs/01 (voice) or docs/10
(sourcing). `format` goes in the ledger exactly as the slug in brackets.

## Monday count (monday-count), Monday morning
Hook: "We checked N [things the buyer has]. M of them [do X]." Our own count, done this week,
about the buyer's stack, not a vendor's survey. Body: what we counted and how, in two sentences;
what the number means for a firm of 10 to 250 in one paragraph; what to check at your place in
one line. No link. Card: plainlist or grid with the count as the headline.
Example hook: "We checked the eight tools a 40-person accountancy already pays for. Six of them have an AI assistant switched on. Two of them are being used."

## One task, one hour (one-task), Tuesday morning
Hook: the task, named as the reader names it. "The Monday supplier reconciliation, done with the assistant you already have." Body: numbered. 1 what to attach. 2 what to type, in quotation marks. 3 what good looks like, so you can tell. 4 what goes wrong the first time. Ends after step 4. No question. Card: grid. Destination: /prompts or none.

## The correction (correction), Wednesday morning
Hook: the belief, then the correction. "Copilot is not a model. It picks one, and you can now pick it yourself." Body: where the belief comes from, why it is wrong, with a source opened this week, and what changes if you drop it. One paragraph each. Card: screenshot of the thing or plainlist. First comment: the caveat.

## The position (position), Thursday morning
Hook: a claim someone could argue with, in the first line. Body per docs/22 section 3: argument, what we would do instead, stop. No link, no question, no first comment. source_publisher=AIFC, archetype=position. Card: house photo card on the reader's workplace, or text-only.

## The week, read (week-read), Friday morning
Hook: "Four things changed in the tools you pay for this week. One needs a decision." Body: four to six items, each publisher, date, claim, why line. The last item is always a UK document (ICO, NCSC, ONS, DSIT, FSB). Tag each publisher through bank/mentions.csv. Card: roundup. Every vendor release of the week lives here and nowhere else unless it passes the Monday test.

## The library (library), floating, twice a month
Hook: a big number and a grouping. "25 prompts, grouped by the moment in the week you need them." Body: the groups, three items each, and one line on how to pick the first. Link to the page that holds the full set. Card: plainlist, dense, the list IS the card. Reposts are the goal.

## The story (story), floating, when one clears docs/22 section 6
Hook: subject, verb, surprising object. Body: what happened, who said what, the one interpretive sentence the reader could not have written, what it means at your scale. Source line. Card: portrait under the docs/17 rule, a picture of the thing, or text-only. Never an empty office.
