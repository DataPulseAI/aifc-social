## ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses: 2023 to 2026
Published 20 July 2026.
https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026

- "The self-reported use of artificial intelligence (AI) in UK businesses with 10 or more employees has increased from around 12% to around 35% since late 2023"
- "the average number of AI technologies used per adopting business rising only modestly, from around 1.4 to around 1.6 since late 2023"
- "large language models were the most widely used among businesses (with 10 or more employees) in June 2026 (18%)"
- "followed by visual content creation (16%), data processing using machine learning (12%), and image processing using machine learning (6%). Robotics (2%)"
- "AI use varies by business size, with 28% of businesses with 0 to 9 employees reporting use of at least one AI technology, compared with 49% of those with 250 or more employees."
- "Almost three-fifths of businesses in the information and communication industry reported use of at least one AI technology."
- "In contrast, industries such as construction show lower levels of AI use overall (13%)."

**Two cautions.** The "58%" for information and communication appears only as a chart
label in Figure 3, not in the body text. Write "almost three-fifths", not 58%. And the
technology percentages and the 12% to 35% trend are for businesses with 10 or more
employees, while the 28% figure is for 0 to 9. Do not mix them in one sentence without
saying so. Keep the word "around" where the ONS uses it.

## McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI
Published 25 August 2026. n=1,719, fieldwork 4 May to 8 June 2026.
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai

- "44 percent now report that AI is scaling across their enterprise, up from 38 percent a year ago."
- "Eighty percent of respondents report that AI has improved their individual productivity, and 50 percent report that AI helps them make better decisions."
- "Thirty-seven percent of respondents attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI use (about the same share as last year)."
- "the proportion of AI high performers ... has remained flat at about 6 percent of all respondents"
- "Fifty-four percent of respondents from organizations with at least $1 billion in annual revenue report scaling AI across the enterprise, compared with one-third of those from smaller organizations."
- "Forty percent of respondents from large organizations ... report scaling AI agents, up from 27 percent last year." / "The share of respondents from smaller organizations reporting scaling remained flat at 22 percent."
- "Nearly nine in ten respondents report regular use of AI in at least one business function."
- "the share of respondents saying their organizations use AI in three or more functions increasing from 51 percent to 56 percent"
- "Just 14 percent of respondents from organizations using AI report that AI contributed to an overall decline in workforce size in the past year."
- "Among mid-level managers and individual contributors, 47 percent say they have experienced one of these negative effects, compared with 31 percent of executives and senior managers."

**Caution.** The 80% is **individual** productivity. Never write it as organisational.

## Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026
"Agents, human agency, and the opportunity for every organization", 5 May 2026.
20,000 workers **using AI** across 10 countries including the UK, fielded 18 Feb to 20 Apr 2026.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization

- "66% of AI users we surveyed say AI has allowed them to spend more time on high-value work"
- "58% say they're producing work they couldn't have a year ago."
- "Only one in four AI users surveyed (26%) say their leadership is clearly and consistently aligned on AI."
- Leader vs employee: feel safe suggesting new ways of working 81% vs 67%; managers create space to experiment 78% vs 59%; reinvention rewarded 21% vs 10%.

**Caution.** The base is AI users, not all employees. Say "AI users" every time.

## NCSC, The hidden risks of shadow AI
Published 7 September 2026. Author Simon B, Senior Cloud Researcher.
https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/blogs/the-hidden-risks-of-shadow-ai

- "nearly three-quarters of employees (71%) reported using AI tools that have not been approved by their employer"
- "Rather than preventing them from using AI, this can mean employees turn to using AI tools that have not been approved by their organisation, introducing new cyber security risks that can be hard to identify."
- "the goal should be to reduce risk rather than assume it can be eliminated"
- "Encouraging open communication about cyber security issues means employees are much less likely to turn to shadow IT services, including shadow AI."

**Caution.** NCSC writes "nearly three-quarters" for 71%. Use 71% or "seven in ten", not
three-quarters. The 71% is NCSC citing a Microsoft study, so it is secondary within NCSC.

## Enterprise Nation Tech Hub, Digital and AI adoption among UK SMEs
Published 9 June 2026. n=1,320 UK micro and small businesses, fieldwork May 2026.
With Google, Sage, Dell Technologies and Square.
https://www.enterprisenation.com/learn-something/one-in-five-small-businesses-regularly-use-ai-new-enterprise-nation-research-finds/

- 21% use AI regularly; of which 6% have it built into daily work across the business and 15% use it regularly for specific tasks
- Barriers: cost of software and setup 53%; lack of skills 46%; data protection or privacy 38%; lack of time to learn and set up 37%; cyber security risk 33%; uncertainty whether it pays off 32%; lack of trusted advice 30%
- "only one in five of the UK's 5.7 million small businesses are using AI regularly"

## Stanford HAI, AI Index Report 2026
Published April 2026. No day-level date is printed; the companion piece
"Inside the AI Index: 12 Takeaways" is dated 13 April 2026.
https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report

- "Organizational AI adoption continued to rise in 2025, up to 88% of surveyed organizations, though AI agent use remains early."
- "Generative AI is now used in at least one business function at 70% of organizations"
- "Generative AI reached 53% adoption in three years, faster than the personal computer or the internet."
- Framing from the hub page: "a widening gap between what AI can do and how prepared we are to manage it"

**Cautions.** Write "published April 2026", no more precise. The widely quoted inference
cost figure is from the **2025** edition and is **not** in the 2026 report; do not use it
in a 2026-dated post. The "widening gap" line is about preparedness to *manage* AI, not
readiness to deploy it.

## Things not to say

- Do not quote ONS "58%" as a stated figure. Say "almost three-fifths".
- Do not say three-quarters of employees use unapproved AI. It is 71%.
- Do not attribute any inference-cost figure to the 2026 AI Index.
- Do not cite the SME Digital Adoption Taskforce update for adoption statistics.
- Do not say McKinsey found 80% productivity gains for organisations. It is individual.
- Do not say 66% of employees save time. The base is AI users surveyed.
