## Monday 29 September. The comparison sheet.
**Format** single image, `out/asked-vs-answered.png` · **Mechanism** the side-by-side pair
(Knight, 75 reposts against 83 comments, the most forwarded shape in the reference set)
**Intention** be the thing a manager forwards to their team without writing a covering note.
The forward is the point. Nothing in it needs a click to have been worth the read.

> Most bad AI answers are bad questions.
>
> Same tool, same document, thirty seconds apart. The only thing that changes is what you typed. Here are six pairs, and the right-hand side of each is a line you can paste today.
>
> **The summary.** Not "summarise this document". Try: summarise this for a finance director with five minutes, keep every figure and date exactly as written.
>
> **The meeting.** Not "what were the actions". Try: list every action as a table, owner, action in their own words, date or trigger, and mark any action where no owner was named. That last clause is the one that earns its place. It finds the work nobody has picked up.
>
> **The contract.** Not "is there anything risky in here". Try: give me the ten questions a careful buyer should ask before signing, hardest first, quoting the clause each one comes from. A summary tells you what a document says. Ten hard questions tell you what it costs you.
>
> **The rewrite.** Not "make this shorter". Try: cut this to 200 words for someone who has not seen it, then list anything you removed that they might still need. The second half is the safety net.
>
> **The gaps.** Not "fill in the rest as best you can". Try: where my notes do not cover a heading, write [GAP: what is missing] rather than writing something plausible. Asked to mark its guesses, it marks them. Not asked, it does not.
>
> **The file.** Not a tidied description of the spreadsheet. The actual spreadsheet, mess and all. The version you clean up first is already an interpretation, and it is where most wrong answers start.
>
> None of that is clever. It is the difference between an answer you rewrite and one you send.
>
> The full twenty-five are written out at aiforcompanies.co.uk/prompts, free, no email.

Hook 38 characters, statement. Body 1,960 characters. No question close, no hashtags, no
mention of what we sell.

## Wednesday 1 October. The photo carousel. Blocked.
**Format** nine slides, `carousels/2026-09-30-seven-habits.json` · **Mechanism** the photograph
carousel (Gray, 460 reactions, 253 comments) · **Intention** the profile stops looking like a
publisher for one post and looks like a person.

Built and rendering. It cannot ship, because it needs eight real photographs and there are
none. Each slide currently renders a dashed panel naming the file it wants. Nothing stock goes
on this and nothing ships on a placeholder.

What it needs, in `viren/assets/photos/`:

| File | What it should be |
|---|---|
| `01-desk-morning.jpg` | wherever the work actually starts. Not staged |
| `02-paper-pile.jpg` | the physical version of the mess we tell people to attach |
| `03-meeting-room.jpg` | a real room with real people in it, backs are fine |
| `04-whiteboard.jpg` | handwriting, ideally mid-argument |
| `05-contract.jpg` | paper on a table, no readable client detail |
| `06-spreadsheet.jpg` | a screen at an angle, nothing identifiable on it |
| `07-template.jpg` | anything that shows a house way of doing something |
| `08-reading.jpg` | Viren, reading, not looking at the camera |

Portrait or square beats landscape. Phone photographs are correct for this format; a
photographer would make it worse.
