# 01. Most of the job was this list

Profile post. Card: `viren/out/01-most-of-the-job.png` (spec in `01-most-of-the-job.json`).
Shape: the then/now list. Model: Hassid's plain list (1,306, 2,617) for the save, Heather's

Why this one and not the licence audit: the audit is a task for a buyer. This is a story about
the reader. Anyone who has worked in an office recognises the left column, and the right column
is six prompts they can use in the next hour. Save comes from the prompts, share comes from
"this was my job too", and the authority comes from where the left column happened.

## Post

Five years at JP Morgan. Most of the job was this list.

Two spreadsheets that should have matched, checked line by line on a Friday night. A 40-page report boiled down to the one paragraph the MD would actually read. Meeting notes turned into actions that nobody owned. The same client update, rewritten a third time because the tone was off.

I was good at it. I also thought it was the work.

Every line in the left column is now a ten-minute task with the assistant your company already pays for. Not a new tool. Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, whichever one is already sitting in your taskbar.

The right column is what you type. That is the whole trick. Attach the file, type the sentence, check the answer.

The gap has never been the software. It is that nobody sits down with a team, on their own files, and shows them the right column.

That is what I left to do.

If you recognise the left column, the right one is for you. Try one this week.
