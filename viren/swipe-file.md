## What the strong accounts never do, and what Viren's own feed shows

They never open with "Most people". Never stack single lines eight deep. Never end on a
question by default (Hassid 0 of 38, Miller 0 of 28, Isenberg 0 of 5). The two that harvest
comments with a giveaway (Donnelly, Hills) have 300k to 1.2M followers and can carry the
penalty; we cannot.

Viren's own last month (3,693 followers, read 21 Sep 2026): the Convo #01 story did 58 and the
hiring post 36; the same Convu pitch pasted into five groups did 4 to 9 each. The pattern is
already visible on his own profile: a room and a story beat a pitch, every time.

## The rule for the profile, in one line

A post runs if a stranger would save the image without reading the post, and a colleague
would forward the post without reading the image. Both, or it does not run.

## Design principles applied to the cards (UI read, 21 Sep 2026)

From Morphica, "LinkedIn carousel best practices for 2026" (8 April 2026): text first, one
idea per image, headline three to eight words if there is one, high contrast, one or two
accents on a consistent base, generous margins, saves and reposts as the measure. Applied here:
1080x1350 at a 12px floor when drawn at feed width; one type family per card; at most three
colours plus greys; nothing on the image that the post already says; the file template carries
no headline at all because the file is the headline.
