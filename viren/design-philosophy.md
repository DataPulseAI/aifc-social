# Working Notes

A visual philosophy for Viren's personal marks, written 22 September 2026. It replaces Quiet
Inventory, which was a company's diagnosis rendered as a dark panel and therefore the wrong
voice for a person's own page.

## The movement

Working Notes holds that the most trustworthy surface is the one somebody has already written
on. Not a brand panel, not a stage, but a page: ruled, plain, faintly used. Its authority comes
from ordinariness rendered with unreasonable care, the sense that every rule was measured, every
margin held, every mark set at the same weight by someone who could not leave a half-pixel
misalignment alone. The work does not announce. It offers. A reader should feel they have been
handed a page from somebody's notebook rather than shown an advertisement, and the difference
is made entirely by restraint.

## Space and form

The ruling is the structure and the subject at once. Lines run edge to edge at a single
unvarying pitch, so the eye reads rhythm before it reads anything else, and so that the empty
regions are evidently part of the page rather than a gap in it. A margin rule stands where a
margin rule stands on real paper, one axis only. Everything else sits within the ruling and
obeys it. A wide reserved area at the left is not absence but a held breath; anything that
drifts into it is destroyed by what sits on top.

## Colour and material

Paper first. A warm off-white ground doing the work a dark panel cannot, because dark reads as
a company and light reads as a person. Ink for the statement. One green for the turn, and a
paler version of the same green for the few marks that suggest entries already written. The
palette is closed at four values and calibrated over many passes, because the marks must look
handled rather than printed, present rather than decorative.

## Scale and rhythm

Two registers and nothing between them. A statement large enough to read across a room, and a
monospaced whisper for the specifics, which is where the relatability lives: naming the four
ordinary jobs does more than any adjective could. Line breaks are chosen for sense rather than
left to the container, and the second line lands in green so the sentence turns rather than
stops. First person throughout. The word I is not a risk on a personal page, it is the point.

## Composition and hierarchy

Ruling, then statement, then the small print, in that order and felt without explanation. The
marked entries sit clear of every word, to the right of the text column, so nothing ever
collides. Optical alignment beats mathematical alignment wherever they disagree.

## The standard

Master-level execution here means a page that survives inspection at full size: every rule the
same weight, every margin true, no mark that cannot say why it is there. If an element is doing
nothing, it is removed, and the page is better for the removal. Nothing on it should look
designed. All of it should be.
