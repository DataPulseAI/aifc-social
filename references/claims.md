# Platform claims we may quote

Verified 23 September 2026 against primary or named secondary sources. Every figure in
`SYSTEM.md` or in a post comes from this table or from our own bank. If a number is not here, it
is not quoted. Add a row before using a new one.

| Document carousels rank first with 1.39x reach and 1.30x engagement (attributed to van der Blom 2025) | corrected | The multipliers are AuthoredUp's: documents generate 39% more reach and 30% more engagement than the average post, 3M+ posts, personal profiles, March 2025 to February 2026. | Sweet spot is 8 to 12 slides | corrected | 8 to 10 slides perform best (van der Blom 2025 via Mercer-Mackay); 6 to 8 with substance (AuthoredUp); engagement drops after slide 10 (Dataslayer) | https://mercermackay.com/thinking/blog/a-leaders-guide-to-the-linkedin-algorithm-what-the-data-says/ | 2025 |
