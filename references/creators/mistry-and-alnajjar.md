The seven post types he attributes to Donnelly's £10M brand, with the goal of each: 1 Awareness (proven ideas, reach new people). 2 Education (teach the ICP, help them decide). 3 Personal story (trust). 4 Proof of work (methodology delivers; followers to leads). 5 Building in public (honest updates while still in it). 6 Lead magnet (useful thing for an email). 7 Newsjacking (trending news tied back to what you do). "You don't need all 7 every week, but you need them in rotation. Every post has to have its own goal."
Read for us: this is the clearest statement of the inform / entertain / retain split the brief asked for, from inside the industry. Our page has been running type 7 (newsjacking) and type 2 (education) only. Types 3, 4 and 5 are structurally closed to a company page with no delivery history, which is exactly why the personal profile has to carry them. "Every post has its own goal" is the line to build the scoring on.
