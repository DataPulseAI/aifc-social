# Justin Welsh (solo creator, "The Saturday Solopreneur" newsletter, low-ticket courses), captured 23 Sep 2026
Post archetypes: Short, complete lessons on building a one-person business, a counter-intuitive claim followed by the reasoning, and newsletter essays cut into posts. Nothing in the post depends on a click: the post is the lesson and the link is the appendix. At his peak he posted five times a day across two platforms.
Hook shapes: A flat declarative first line that states the whole idea ("To build a business on LinkedIn, you need better content"), numbered frameworks, before and after contrast. No questions, no "comment YES".
Visual pattern: Widely described as one image, one sentence, the key phrase highlighted in yellow on a plain off-white ground, screenshot-like rather than designed (`top-content/img/tc-5.jpg` is one, 1,060 characters of body). Not verifiable from his own domain, which serves essay text only.
Funnel mechanic: "Half an hour after I share a piece of content, I follow-up with a link to an article." The article is free on his site with product mentions woven in. The newsletter promise is one line: every Saturday, one actionable tip. No classic lead magnet; the welcome sequence carries a discounted product. Reported: $4.8 million over four years (October 2023), about 220,000 subscribers (September 2025, third-party estimate).
Transfer to AIFC: Take the value-complete post and the rule that the reader who never clicks still leaves with the lesson; take the single highlighted sentence as a one-line card. Refuse the daily volume, the personal-brand centre and the revenue flexing.
