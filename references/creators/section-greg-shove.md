# Greg Shove and Section (CEO of Section, the New York AI upskilling firm behind the AI Proficiency Report), captured 23 Sep 2026
The direct business analogue: AI upskilling sold to companies, with original research as the lead magnet.
Post archetypes: One benchmark figure stated as a crisis, then the diagnosis. Section publishes one large workforce survey a year and mines it for months of posts, blog pieces, live "Executive Briefing" events and a press release. Number first, self-perception gap second, cause third. June 2025: 10% proficient, 54% think they are, 43% of companies train (5,013 knowledge workers, US, UK and Canada). June 2026: 5.5% proficient, 73.5% Experimenters, 20.9% Novices (5,026 US knowledge workers), with an agent angle (under 10% can define an agent, 16% use one).
Hook shapes: "Most people are terrible at using AI [new data]", "The AI Proficiency Crisis", "Enterprise AI Investments Jump While AI Skills Flatline". Always a gap between spend and skill, or confidence and score.
Visual pattern: Long report landing page on a near-black ground with a portrait of Shove, then sixteen numbered chart frames, each a single horizontal bar or a two-bar comparison, the figure set large, the label small, one accent colour, no gridlines. Each chart proves the one sentence beside it.
Funnel mechanic: The report is gated behind a HubSpot form. A second gate offers an individual assessment. The sales bridge is explicit in the June 2025 blog: "we can run the same benchmark for your team of 50+ people", against the rest of the workforce and your competitors, with a direct email to Shove and a "Book a demo" button.
Transfer to AIFC: Take one owned figure restated for a year (the Monday count), and the benchmark-your-team bridge: our scoping survey of attendees is already that, and Resource 05 (the team readiness check) makes it a self-check the buyer runs first. Refuse the crisis framing, the enterprise scale claims, and gating the report itself: we publish the knowledge free and gate only the working document. Section's mechanism works because they own a number nobody else has; ours has to be a count we did ourselves, not their survey quoted.
